7x Mr. Olympian Arnold Schwarzenegger

Additonally, Arnold Schwarzenegger is widely considered a legend in the bodybuilding scene as he has been a standing figure for decades even after his bodybuilding years and he even created his own competition, that being the Arnold Classic!
